The Process of Making Timnet Gebru's Website

Creating a website for Timnet Gebru involved several key steps, from planning and design to development and deployment. Here's an overview of the process:

1. Planning and Research

During this step we came together as a group to discuss who did what. Like who did the process page or who did the about page. We also talked about the design and overall devolpment in the start of assinments.

2. Design

As a class we separated into groups to work on our different projects. And our group went into figma createing a offical prototype for our website. then we walked it over and created a amazing start.
