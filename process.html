The Process of Making Timnet Gebru's Website

Creating a website for Timnet Gebru involved several key steps, from planning and design to development and deployment. Here's an overview of the process:

1. Planning and Research

During this step we came together as a group to discuss who did what. Like who did the process page or who did the about page. We also talked about the design and overall devolpment in the start of assinments.

2. Design

As a class we separated into groups to work on our different projects. Our group went into figma, creating an offical prototype for our website. Then we talked it over and created an amazing start.

3. Development

After we had our design we started to create the website. We used html and css to create a website for our innovaotor. We also logged into Github and Google Sheets to create charts and much more for the website. This added so much and this got us much closer to finishing the website.

4. Looking Good

Once the website was functional, we focused on making it visually appealing and user friendly. We redid the overall design, and made the design easier and more modern. With the layout we ensured that the website was responsive across different devices.
